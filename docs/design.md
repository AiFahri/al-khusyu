# Interface Direction

AlKhusyu combines institutional trust with an editorial presentation suited to
education, achievement, and community stories.

## Principles

- Establish the foundation or education unit clearly in the first viewport.
- Use hierarchy and spacing to make long institutional content easy to scan.
- Let photography explain real programs, facilities, and activity outcomes.
- Keep actions explicit and avoid decorative controls that imply behavior.
- Preserve a consistent route and component structure across education units.

## Responsive Behavior

Navigation, content grids, media, and typography must remain usable from compact
mobile screens through wide desktop layouts. Fixed-format media should have a
stable aspect ratio so loading and content changes do not shift surrounding
sections.

## Media

Images must be approved for public use, compressed for their rendered size, and
paired with alternative text that describes the visible subject or functional
purpose. Decorative imagery should use empty alternative text.

## Interaction

Focus states, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion preferences, and readable
touch targets are part of the design contract rather than post-release polish.
