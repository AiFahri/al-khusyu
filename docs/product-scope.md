# Product Scope

## Purpose

AlKhusyu provides one public entry point for understanding Yayasan Pendidikan
dan Sosial Al-Khusyu', its education units, programs, activities, achievements,
and institutional direction.

## Audiences

- Prospective families comparing education pathways.
- Current students, parents, teachers, and foundation staff.
- Alumni, partners, and community members following institutional activity.
- Search visitors looking for a specific Al-Khusyu' unit or program.

## Core Journeys

1. Learn the foundation's identity, mission, and history.
2. Explore an education unit and its facilities, activities, and direction.
3. Discover a program and open its detail narrative.
4. Browse current activities or achievement stories.
5. Continue to the relevant institutional contact or live service.

## Explicit Exclusions

The frontend is not an admissions system, learning-management system, student
information system, payment service, private staff portal, or content-management
backend. Those capabilities require separate authorization, privacy, data, and
operational designs.
